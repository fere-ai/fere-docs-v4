<Warning>
  **Market Pulse is currently unavailable.** The in-app Market Pulse page and new scheduled Market Pulse summaries (email or Telegram delivery) are turned off. Existing Market Pulse schedules have been paused.
</Warning>

When it was live, Market Pulse scanned crypto social channels and delivered a structured digest for a chosen time window — on demand or on a schedule.

## What still works

- **Research / Chats** at `/research` — chat with Fere for market questions and research.
- **Strategies** — scheduled `CHAT` strategies continue to run.
- Personal / user-specific Telegram summaries (where enabled) are separate from Market Pulse and are not covered by this pause.
