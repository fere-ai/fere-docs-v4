<Warning>
  **Market Pulse is deprecated.** New and resumed scheduled digests (`SUMMARY`) are rejected with the message **Market Pulse is deprecated.** The in-app `/market-pulse` route redirects to [Research](https://fereai.xyz/research). Existing Market Pulse schedules are hard-stopped and will not run.
</Warning>

Market Pulse previously scanned crypto social channels and delivered Instant or scheduled (email / Telegram) digests. That product surface is shut down.

## What still works

| Keep using | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **[Research](/agent)** | Ask Fere agents for market narratives, token research, and live chat. |
| **[Strategies](/strategies)** | Recurring `CHAT` schedules and strategy automation (unchanged). |
| **User-specific Telegram summaries** | Personal `USER_SPECIFIC_SUMMARY` schedules are **not** part of this cut. |

## What stopped

- Creating a new Market Pulse schedule (`schedule_type=SUMMARY`) — API returns **400** `Market Pulse is deprecated.`
- Resuming a paused Market Pulse schedule — same **400** response.
- Recurring SUMMARY checker runs — leftover rows are cancelled with reason `product_surface_disable_market_pulse`.
- Navigating to `/market-pulse` in the webapp — redirects to `/research` (product surfaces gate).

Pausing or disabling a leftover SUMMARY agent remains allowed so you can clear UI state.
