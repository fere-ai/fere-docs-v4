Found something worth showing off — a trade, a research breakdown, a strategy result? Share turns that conversation into a public page anyone can view.

## Sharing a Chat

Click the **Share** icon on any conversation. Fere generates a public link and opens a panel where you can:

- **Copy the link** to paste anywhere.
- **Post directly** to X, Farcaster, Telegram, or Discord with one click.

<Info icon="eye">
  **What a viewer sees.** A shared link opens a read-only, static view of that conversation — anyone with the link can read it, but they can't reply or use your wallet. A **"Go to app"** link on the page lets them start their own chat with Fere.
</Info>

## Explore

**Explore** ([fereai.xyz/explore](https://fereai.xyz/explore)) is the public directory of shared chats — a scrolling grid of what people have published, newest first. Anyone can browse it without signing in; it's a way to see real Fere research and trades before you connect a wallet.

<Tip>
  **Sharing is opt-in.** Only conversations you explicitly share appear on a public link or in Explore. Chats you haven't shared stay private to your account, and turning on **Private chats by default** in [Settings](/settings) keeps new conversations out of your history entirely.
</Tip>
