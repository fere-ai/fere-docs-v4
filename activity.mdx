Every time Fere completes an action on your behalf — a swap, a transfer, a strategy trade, a limit order firing — it's logged here, so you can see exactly what happened and when.

## The Activity Feed

Open **Activity** to see your full notification history, grouped into **Today**, **Yesterday**, and **Older**. Each card shows the type of event, the tokens and amounts involved, and its outcome (success or failure).

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Trades & swaps" icon="right-left">
    Buys, sells, and swaps executed by you or by a strategy.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Transfers" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square">
    Funds moving in or out of your wallet or a strategy account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Limit orders & TP/SL" icon="bullseye">
    Orders placed, filled, or triggered — including take-profit and stop-loss legs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Strategy runs" icon="clock">
    Notifications from your [strategies](/strategies) completing a scheduled cycle.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Filtering

Narrow the feed with the time-range tabs at the top: **All**, **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, or pick a **custom date range**.

<Tip>
  **Deep links land you here.** Notifications and run-history links elsewhere in Fere (like on a [strategy's detail page](/strategies#strategy-detail-page)) can open straight to the relevant activity.
</Tip>
