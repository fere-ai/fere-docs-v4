Strategies let you automate any instruction — no matter how complex — and have Fere run it on repeat. Your strategies run on autopilot — buying, rebalancing, and watching the market while you do literally anything else. Describe what you want once, and Fere handles the rest every single cycle.

## What Are Strategies?

A strategy is an automated instruction that runs on repeat. You describe it in the chat at **/app**, tell Fere how often to run it, and sit back. Every cycle, the agent goes through the full reasoning process — scanning, analyzing, deciding, and (if conditions are met) trading.

And here's what makes it powerful: **Fere remembers.**

## Gets Smarter Over Time

Every time a strategy runs, it carries forward what it found before. By day 7 of an hourly scan, your agent has a rich picture of every token it analyzed, every score it gave, every trade it made or skipped. It can spot things a stateless bot never would — like a token that scored 85 three days ago, dropped to 60 yesterday, and just came back to 82. That's a recovery signal, and Fere catches it because it was watching.

<Tip>
  **Context That Compounds.** Fere distills past runs into the signals that actually matter — not a wall of logs, but a smart research notebook that makes every cycle more informed than the last.
</Tip>

## How to Create a Strategy

Go to the main chat at **/app** and describe what you want to automate. The AI sets it up and it appears in **/strategies**.

<Info>
  **Ongoing trading bots need a saved script before they'll schedule.**

  For a Polymarket, Hyperliquid, or on-chain trading bot you want running on autopilot, ask Fere to build and save it first (for example, *"build this as a script"*) so it writes a runnable script to the strategy's workspace. Fere then schedules it in **script mode**, rerunning that exact script every cycle instead of re-reasoning from scratch each time.

  One-off research summaries and digests don't need this — they stay in **agent mode**, where Fere reasons fresh each cycle. But if you ask Fere to *"schedule this"* for a trading bot before a script exists, Fere won't silently fall back to agent mode — it'll **decline to create the schedule** and ask you to build the script first, then try scheduling again.
</Info>

<Warning>
  **Deployed strategies and the chat that created them are separate workspaces.**

  When you deploy a strategy from a chat, Fere copies the logic into a **dedicated workspace** bound to that strategy on **/strategies/[id]**. The original thread at **/app** and the live strategy **do not share files or edits** after deployment.

  | Where you work | What it affects |
  | --- | --- |
  | **Strategy chat** on **/strategies/[id]** | The **live** scheduled strategy — its workspace, scripts, and schedule. Use this (or **Accept** on a recommendation) to change what runs on autopilot. |
  | **Main chat** at **/app** | A **separate** workspace. You can keep iterating there, but that work creates a **new** strategy — it does **not** update the one already deployed. |

  **Rule of thumb:** To change a running strategy, open its detail page and use Strategy chat there — not the conversation where you first built it.
</Warning>

## Farming Strategies

Alongside the templates above, **Start a Strategy** also surfaces a **Farming** preset — a ready-made, always-on automation for Polymarket. Unlike a custom strategy, you don't write an instruction: you flip a switch and tune a few numeric settings. Once started, an active Farming strategy also shows up at the top of **Your Strategies**.

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Polymarket Farming" icon="seedling">
    Auto-trade small buys and sells on Polymarket to build up volume, on a timer you control.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Swing Trading and Day Trading farming strategies have been retired and are no longer available to start, pause, or resume. If you ran one previously, its past trades still appear in your Portfolio activity under their original label, but the strategy's detail page is no longer reachable.
</Note>

Each preset gets its own page at **/strategies/farming/[kind]**, opened by clicking its card. A status pill shows exactly where it stands, since every Farming strategy trades from its own dedicated wallet and isn't actually running just because it's switched on:

- **Inactive** — the switch is off.
- **Setting up…** — switched on, but its dedicated wallet is still being created.
- **Setup needed** — the wallet exists, but funding or venue setup isn't finished yet, so it isn't trading.
- **Active** — funded, set up, and trading.

### Polymarket Farming

Requires a Polymarket wallet with approvals set up first — the page prompts you to **Go to Polymarket setup** if you haven't. Once set up, you can tune:

- **Trade size (USD)** — dollars spent per buy.
- **Pause below balance (USD)** — stop buying if your Polymarket wallet drops below this.
- **How often to buy / sell** — minutes between attempts.
- **Advanced settings** (collapsed by default) — delay before the first buy and first sell, and how many market pages to scan per round.

Fields come prefilled with suggested values. Flip the toggle on to start — changes take effect within a minute. Turning it off keeps your settings saved for next time.

### Managing a Farming Strategy

The Farming detail page's sidebar mirrors the regular Strategy Detail Page's, minus Strategy chat and Files — there's no free-form chat here, since Farming strategies run on fixed logic instead of an instruction you write. It shows:

- **Runs** — every automated cycle.
- **Transactions** — buys, sells, and other trades it executed.

A **Strategy Wallet** row is pinned above these tabs, showing this strategy's own balance, a **Fund** button, and an **Open portfolio** link for withdrawals and full history — see [Strategy Wallets](#strategy-wallets) below.

On mobile, tap the **Strategy** button in the top app bar to open the same sidebar as a bottom sheet.

## Strategy Wallets

Farming strategies (Polymarket Farming) and scheduled strategies that trade on-chain each get their own **dedicated strategy wallet** — a separate EVM and Solana address from your main wallet. The strategy's trading capital or Polymarket balance lives in this wallet, not your main one.

A script-mode strategy that only reads data — a research digest or a threshold scanner with nothing to buy or sell — is the exception: it can run straight from your main wallet, skipping the fund-and-activate checklist below entirely. Fere checks the script itself to decide this, not what you typed to create it — anything that could move funds, or that Fere can't confirm is read-only, always gets its own isolated wallet instead.

<Info>
  **Started your strategy a while back?** Strategies created before dedicated wallets launched still run on your main wallet. On those, a dismissible **"Strategies v2 is here"** banner appears on the Farming card/detail page or schedule detail page, letting you know it isn't isolated yet. For a Farming strategy, stop it and start it again to move it onto its own wallet automatically; for a scheduled strategy, pause it and recreate it from chat.
</Info>

### Getting a strategy ready to trade

Right after you start a Farming strategy, its detail page briefly shows **"Setting up your strategy"** — its dedicated wallet is being created (usually under a minute) — with a preview of the steps still to come: add funds, set up the venue, and activate. Once the wallet is ready, this gives way to the checklist itself.

Before a new strategy can trade, its detail page (or Farming page) shows a **"Get this strategy ready"** checklist:

- **Fund wallet** — deposit into the strategy's own wallet.
- **Venue setup** — for Hyperliquid strategies, funding and enabling the account are combined into one step: depositing automatically triggers Hyperliquid setup in the background (you'll see *"Finishing trading setup in the background — usually about a minute"*). For Polymarket, setup and funding stay as two separate steps.
- **Activate strategy** — a final switch to start it trading.

Only the next incomplete step is clickable at a time. Once everything is done, the checklist collapses into a single line — *"This strategy is set up and ready to trade"* — with a **Details** toggle to re-expand the individual steps if you need to check something.

### Funding a strategy wallet

Tap **Fund** (or **Fund Hyperliquid** / **Fund Polymarket**) to open the fund modal:

- Pick any token you hold on any supported chain — the same chain/token picker used elsewhere in Fere.
- A **Max** shortcut fills in your full available balance for the selected token.
- A live conversion preview shows what you'll deposit.
- Minimums: **$50** for a Hyperliquid strategy wallet, **$5** for a Polymarket strategy wallet. Entering an amount below the minimum shows an inline "Minimum $X required" hint rather than just disabling the button.

Fere swaps and bridges as needed, then credits the strategy's wallet or venue balance. A "processing" banner stays up until the deposit settles.

### Where you see it

- **Wallet panel** — a **Strategies** section lists a card for each of your active strategies, showing its balance and **Fund** / **Withdraw** buttons.
- **Strategy detail page** — a compact wallet row at the top of the right sidebar (see [Right sidebar](#right-sidebar-positions-transactions-runs-files) above) shows balance and **Fund**, with an **Open portfolio** link for everything else, including **Withdraw**.
- **Portfolio** — select this strategy's account to fund, withdraw, or review its positions and history — see [Portfolio](/portfolio).

### Withdrawing funds

Use **Withdraw** on the strategy wallet card to move spot tokens back to your main wallet — a **Max** shortcut fills in the full withdrawable balance. The strategy keeps running; withdrawing does not pause or stop it.

Open Hyperliquid or Polymarket positions aren't included in Withdraw — close or redeem them first through the strategy's own venue flow (the same way you would from the main wallet), then withdraw the resulting spot balance.

<Warning>
  **Pausing or stopping a strategy does not return its funds.** The strategy wallet and any open positions stay exactly as they are — pausing only stops new scheduled runs. Use **Withdraw** whenever you want funds back in your main wallet.
</Warning>

### Archived strategies

If a strategy is cancelled while its wallet still holds a balance, it moves to a **Closed · still funded** group in the Accounts rail on [Portfolio](/portfolio). Closed accounts only offer **Withdraw** (no more funding), and the row disappears automatically once the balance is fully withdrawn.

## Strategy Detail Page

Click any strategy card to open its detail page at **/strategies/[id]**. This is your command center for one strategy — what it has done, what it traded, what it produced, and what you want it to do next.

### Fund performance

For strategies with venue-reported trading data (most active strategies — spot, Hyperliquid, or Polymarket), the briefing card leads with a **Fund performance** report instead of going straight to operational stats. Newer or research-only strategies without enough data yet fall back to the plain [Operations](#operations) summary described below.

- **Coverage badge** — **Full performance data**, **Partial (Hyperliquid)**, **Mixed venues**, **Research only**, or **Performance unavailable**, telling you how complete the numbers below are. A bullet list under the badge explains any gaps, including which venue's figures are missing and why (its API didn't respond).
- **Headline** — a one-line summary of how the fund is doing.
- **Two top tiles**:
  - **Strategy equity** (Hyperliquid-only strategies — live margin used plus unrealized PnL), **Strategy exposure** (mixed-venue strategies — total holdings value across venues), or **Strategy cost basis** (spot-only, no live pricing available) — the label always matches what the number actually measures, it's never guessed from the strategy type.
  - **Open PnL** — current unrealized profit/loss, when available.
- **PnL reported by each venue** — one card per venue that traded (Tokens, Hyperliquid, Polymarket), each showing **Realized** PnL and, when the venue supports it, **Unrealized** PnL, plus a "via [source]" credit line. Every figure comes straight from that venue's own API rather than being calculated by Fere. If a venue's API is unavailable, its card says so instead of showing a stale or fabricated number. There's deliberately no combined total across venues, since each one reports over a different time window (for example, token PnL covers the last year while Hyperliquid and Polymarket report since the position opened) — summing them would imply a coverage guarantee Fere can't make.
- **Performance commentary** and **Mandate adherence** — narrative write-ups of how the strategy has performed and whether it's still doing what you asked for.
- **Fund exposure** — a list of current open positions (token/market, chain, allocation %, side and leverage for perps), each with its value and unrealized PnL.
- **Risk & outlook** — a short note on risk and what to watch going forward, when Fere has something to flag.

### Operations

Below Fund performance (or at the top of the card, for strategies without a fund report yet) is an **Operations** section:

- **Health status** — whether the strategy is on track, degraded, blocked, paused, or research-only.
- **Key metrics** — run success rate, trade outcomes, and next scheduled run.
- **Since last update** — bullet changes since the previous briefing (incremental window).
- **Top positions** — largest holdings when portfolio data is available, shown here only for strategies without a Fund performance report above (otherwise see **Fund exposure**, hidden for research-only agents).
- **Narrative sections** — at-a-glance, recent performance, and since-last-update prose grounded in verified run and trade data.

A compatibility markdown summary is still stored for exports and older clients. The briefing refreshes after each completed run. If a run just finished, you may briefly see *"Updating briefing after the latest run…"* while new content is generated.

### Recommendations

**Recommended actions** sit in the same briefing card, directly under Operations' metrics (and positions, when shown). They are separate from the narrative briefing: short, actionable cards tuned for *what to do next*, not a prose recap of the last run.

#### What you see on each card

Each recommendation includes:

- **Priority** — **Now** (urgent), **Soon**, or **Later**, so you know what to tackle first.
- **Category** — **Execution**, **Risk**, **Logic**, **Config**, or **Research**.
- **Title and why** — a plain-language summary and the reason Fere surfaced it.
- **Steps** — one to five direct imperatives (for example, *"Extend the schedule for 3 more runs"* or *"Set a take profit at 5% above entry"*). Steps are written for you as the owner, not as developer instructions.
- **Evidence** (when shown) — pointers to the run, trade, signal, or script behavior that supported the suggestion.

Fere shows up to five recommendations at a time. If nothing new is warranted, the section may be empty.

#### What you can do

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept** | Sends the recommendation (title + steps) into **Strategy chat** at the bottom of the page. Fere treats it like your own instruction and can update the strategy workspace, schedule settings, or trading logic as needed. |
| **Decline** | Removes the card from your list. Declined items are remembered — they will not reappear after the next automatic refresh or when you tap **Refresh recommendations**. |
| **Refresh recommendations** | Enqueues a new generation pass on demand. The link is enabled when recommendations are **stale** (a run finished after the last recommendations update). |

While a new pass is running after a completed run, you may see *"Updating recommendations after the latest run…"* with no cards yet. That is normal; reload or wait a moment for the job to finish.

<Tip>
  **Accept vs. typing in chat.** Accept is a shortcut: it pre-fills Strategy chat with the same steps you would paste yourself. You can still edit the message before sending, or ask follow-ups in the same thread.
</Tip>

#### How recommendations are generated (high level)

Recommendations are produced by a **dedicated background job** that runs in parallel with the executive briefing — not copied from the briefing text.

1. **Gather evidence** — Fere assembles a structured snapshot of your strategy: recent and older scheduled runs, trade outcomes, holdings, error patterns (with extra weight on *recent* failures), your active strategy text, saved workspace scripts when present, optimizer metrics if enabled, and your past Strategy chat follow-ups.
2. **Detect signals** — Rule-based checks flag themes worth addressing (for example, repeated run failures, stale errors that are already resolved, or script-specific trading context on Hyperliquid or Polymarket).
3. **Generate cards** — An AI model reads only that evidence and outputs structured recommendations. Each card must cite supporting evidence; invented balances, prices, or trade counts are not allowed.
4. **Sanitize for product context** — Post-processing drops generic coding advice (logging, refactoring, *"edit your `.py` file"*). Steps stay in owner language: schedule changes, risk guardrails, notifications, and workspace updates via Fere — aligned with how strategies actually run (saved scripts and `strategy_builder`, not manual IDE edits).
5. **Respect your choices** — Declined recommendations are stored so refresh passes do not suggest the same intent again.

The executive briefing and recommendations share the same trigger (after a completed run) but serve different jobs: the briefing explains *what happened*; recommendations suggest *what to do next*.

### Strategy chat

Below the summary is **Strategy chat** — your follow-up conversation with **this deployed strategy's workspace**. This is the right place to change what runs on the schedule (not the original **/app** thread). Strategy chat is also separate from scheduled runs themselves:

- Ask clarifying questions (*"Why did you skip the last trade?"*)
- Request changes (*"Raise the score threshold to 90 and cap buys at $25"*)
- Pause or stop the strategy in plain language

Older messages load on scroll (newest at the bottom), so long-running strategies stay fast even after dozens of runs and follow-ups.

### Run status bar and input

A sticky bar at the bottom of the page shows:

- **Run count** — how many scheduled cycles have completed.
- **Next run** — when the strategy fires again (hidden while paused).

Just below that, the input bar accepts new instructions. Type `@` to mention another active or paused strategy when you want to reference or coordinate across automations.

### Right sidebar — Positions, Transactions, Runs, Files

On desktop, a sidebar on the right replaces the generic Files panel while you are on a strategy detail page. It stays visible for that strategy. From top to bottom: a status bar (run count, next run, Pause/Resume), a compact **Strategy Wallet** row, a pinned **Last run** summary, then a tabbed pane.

The tabbed pane has four tabs — **Positions**, **Transactions**, **Runs**, **Files**:

| Tab | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Positions** | This strategy's own wallet holdings. |
| **Transactions** | Swaps, buys, sells, and other trades this strategy executed, newest first. Each row links out to the block explorer when a transaction hash is available. |
| **Runs** | Numbered list of every scheduled cycle (`Run #1`, `Run #2`, …) with timestamps. Click a run to open a detail modal with the full agent outcome and any on-chain **Activities** from that cycle. |
| **Files** | Files the agent generated across runs — reports, charts, exports. Click to preview or download. |

The **Strategy Wallet** row shows this strategy's balance with a refresh button, a **Fund** button, and an **Open portfolio** link that opens this strategy's account in [Portfolio](/portfolio) in a new tab. Withdrawing is done from Portfolio, not from this row — see [Where you see it](#where-you-see-it) below.

On mobile, tap the **Strategy** button in the top app bar (it only appears while you're on a strategy page) to open the same tabbed pane in a bottom sheet.

<Tip>
  **Deep-link to a run or message.** Links from notifications and run history can scroll you directly to the relevant chat message or run in the detail page.
</Tip>

<Note>
  **Strategy couldn't load?** If the page can't reach your strategy (session expired, no access, not found, or a temporary error), it shows a full-page message explaining why, with **Retry** and **Back to Strategies** buttons instead of the chat and sidebar.
</Note>

<Info>
  **Pause or stop anytime.** You are always in control. Pause from the Strategies list, or tell the agent in the detail input (*"Pause this strategy until Monday"*). No lock-in, no penalties — the agent stops on the next safe boundary.
</Info>
