The Fere AI iPhone app (`xyz.fereai.app`) uses the same gateway and wallets as the web app. After launch, signed-out users see a welcome screen; signed-in users land in the **revamp shell** with four bottom tabs.

## Sign in

- **Sign in with Apple** is the primary option on the welcome screen.
- **Clerk** handles the session (email, Google, Discord, and wallet options are available from **More sign-in options**).
- OAuth callbacks return to the app; the session JWT is exchanged with the Fere gateway before trading features unlock.
- If the gateway rejects a session, the app prompts you to sign in again.

## Four tabs

| Tab | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Portfolio** | Net worth chart, recent trades, suggestions, and shortcuts to fund or withdraw. |
| **Agents** | Browse the catalog, open agents you run, and start or manage strategies. |
| **Activity** | Ledger-style feed of deposits, trades, and agent events. |
| **Search** | Find tokens and agents across the catalog. |

First-time signed-in users may see a short **onboarding** flow (pick a goal and fund an agent) before the tab bar appears.

## Funding agents

When you start or add money to an agent:

1. Choose amount and network.
2. If your **main wallet** already holds enough on that chain, Fere can move funds into the agent wallet in-app.
3. Otherwise the app shows **external deposit** instructions — EVM and Solana addresses (and QR codes) so you can send crypto from another wallet or exchange. The app can wait for the transfer and show a **Live Activity** on the Lock Screen while funding completes.

Card and Apple Pay rails used on web may appear where the payment gateway supports them for your region; on-chain **external deposit** is always available when in-wallet transfer is not.

## Withdraw

From **Portfolio** or an agent’s running screen, open **Withdraw** to move funds from an agent wallet back to your main wallet. You can require **Face ID** for withdrawals in **Account → Settings**.

## Widgets and Live Activities

Add Fere widgets from the iOS widget gallery:

- **Today’s total** — portfolio snapshot.
- **Week trades** — recent activity count.
- **Running agent** — highlights an active strategy.

During agent funding, a **Live Activity** (and Dynamic Island on supported devices) shows progress until the deposit lands.

## Research and deploy

**Agents → Build** opens **Research** — the same chat-style research surface as before. From a strategy card you can still open the **Deploy** sheet to configure and launch an agent on your account.

## Account

Tap your avatar on **Portfolio** for **Account**: appearance (system / light / dark), withdrawal Face ID, notifications, support links, and sign out.
