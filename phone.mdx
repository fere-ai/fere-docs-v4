On a phone, the [Fere app](https://www.fereai.xyz) in your browser and the Fere iOS app look and work the same way. The screens, buttons and wording match, so these steps apply to both.

## The Three Tabs

A bar at the bottom of the screen has three tabs:

- **Portfolio**: your total, your Fere Wallet and your agents.
- **Agents**: every agent you can start, and the ones you've started.
- **Activity**: every trade, deposit and withdrawal, newest first. A number on the tab shows how many you haven't seen yet.

Tap your **avatar** at the top right to open the account menu. A dot on it means you have unread activity.

## The Account Menu

The account menu shows the email you signed in with, then:

- **Research** and **Points**
- **Refer and Earn**, **Settings** and **Support**
- **Build with MCP** and **API Docs** (see [MCP Support](/mcp))
- **Logout**

The iOS app also has **Appearance**, **Face ID for withdrawals** and **Invite code** in this menu.

## Portfolio

**Portfolio value** is everything across your Fere Wallet and your agents. Under it is your profit or loss, labelled with how it's counted. For example, **realized** means only trades that have closed.

- **Fere Wallet** shows your available cash, with **Add money** and **Withdraw**. Tap the card to open the Fere Wallet screen.
- **Your agents** lists your agents with their value and status: **Running**, **Needs money**, **Stopped** or **Stopped by Fere**. If you have more than five, tap **{n} total** to see them all under Agents › Yours.

### The Fere Wallet Screen

The Fere Wallet screen shows your **Cash**, your profit or loss, and **Withdraw** and **Deposit**. Below that:

- **Positions** lists what the wallet holds. Tap **Sell** on a coin, or **Withdraw** on Cash.
- **Trades** lists the wallet's trades.
- **Orders** lists open limit orders.

The wallet button at the top opens **Addresses and private key** and **Hide balances**. With **Hide balances** on, amounts show as **••••**. Turn it off the same way.

## An Agent's Page

Open an agent from Portfolio or from Agents › Yours to see its wallet:

- The **Agent wallet** card shows its balance, its profit or loss while it runs, and how much is cash and how much is in positions. Use **Add money** to fund it and **Take money out** to move cash back to your Fere Wallet.
- **Positions**, **Trades** and **Orders** show what it holds, what it traded and its open orders. Tap a position to **Sell**, **Withdraw**, **Move to Cash** or **Redeem** it, whichever applies.
- **Hide dust & unverified** hides coins under $2.00 and coins we can't verify. See [Dust and unverified tokens](/wallet#dust-and-unverified-tokens).
- The **•••** button opens **Add money**, **Take money out**, **Settings** and **Stop**. Stopping asks you to confirm first. Nothing is sold: the agent's holdings and its take-profit and stop-loss orders stay in place. For an agent still waiting for money, **Stop** cancels its pending start.

## Agents

- **Discover** lists the agents you can start. Filter by kind (for example **Perps**, **US stocks** or **Crypto**), and sort by **Recommended** or **Best**. An agent you're running, or one you've set up that's waiting for money, isn't listed here; it's under **Yours**. A stopped agent shows in both, so you can start it again from Discover.
- **Yours** groups the agents you've started into **Running**, **Needs money** and **Stopped**. Below them, **Your custom strategies** lists strategies you built in chat.
- **Search** sits under the title. On Discover it matches agent names and what they trade. On Yours it matches your agents' names.

Open an agent to see its **Overview** (performance chart, drawdown, key stats and live trades) and **About** (how it trades, its market, venue, fees, minimum deposit and risk). Then tap **Set up agent** to start it.

## Activity

- Switch between **All**, **Trades** and **Money**. Money covers deposits, withdrawals and moves between your wallets. Trades covers everything else.
- The **filter** button limits the list to **Today**, **Yesterday**, the **Last 7 days** or the **Last 30 days**. Tap **Reset** to show everything again.
- Each row says what happened, which agent did it and when. A failed action is marked **Failed**.
- Tap a row to see its details and **View agent**. Tap **Mark all read** to clear the unread count.
