Everything you need to know about using Fere AI — from starting an agent to trading from your own AI.

## Agents

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a Fere agent?">
    An agent is an automated trader that runs one unique strategy. You pick an agent from the catalogue, fund its wallet, and it trades on its own, day and night. See [Agents](/agents).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change an agent's strategy?">
    **No.** Each agent's strategy is built and maintained by Fere, and its logic can't be edited. Some agents let you adjust a few settings — like amount per trade, leverage, stop loss, or take profit — while others run with fixed settings.

    Want a custom agent or to run your own strategy? [Contact us](/support).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much do I need to start an agent?">
    It depends on the agent — each one has its own minimum, shown on the funding screen. If an agent's minimum is out of reach, its page may suggest a smaller agent with a similar strategy.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does it cost?">
    Fere charges **0.5% of each trade** (0.1% for perps agents). There are no other fees from Fere.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I stop an agent?">
    Open the agent's page and choose **Stop**. It stops making new trades right away. Whatever it holds stays in its wallet, and any take-profit and stop-loss orders it placed stay on. You can sell, withdraw, or **Start again** whenever you like.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why hasn't my agent traded yet?">
    Agents only trade when their strategy signals an opportunity, so quiet stretches are normal — some strategies trade rarely. Settings change the size and exits of each trade, never how often the agent trades.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I lose money?">
    **Yes.** Agents trade real money in volatile markets, and past performance doesn't guarantee future results. Only fund an agent with money you can afford to lose.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Wallet & Funds

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is my money held?">
    Every agent you start has its own wallet, powered by Coinbase CDP Server Wallets. Private keys live inside hardware-isolated Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs). The agent can only trade with its wallet — only you can withdraw. See [Wallet & Security](/wallet).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is Cash?">
    **Cash** is the money in an agent's wallet that isn't currently in a trade, shown in US dollars. Deposits land as Cash, and Cash is what you withdraw.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I add funds?">
    Use **Fund** (or **Fund more**) on the agent's page. The funding screen shows which networks you can send from and the address to send to on each.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I withdraw?">
    On the agent's page, choose **Withdraw**, enter an amount in USD, and the address to send it to. If the agent is holding positions, sell them back to Cash first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## MCP & Research

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where did Research go?">
    Research now runs through the **Fere MCP**. Connect it to Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Hermes, OpenClaw, or any other MCP client to research markets with Fere's tools. See [MCP Support](/mcp).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I trade through the MCP?">
    **Yes.** Through the MCP, your own AI can swap tokens, place limit orders, trade perps and prediction markets, and more — just ask it in plain language.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need approval to use the MCP?">
    **No.** The MCP is live and open to everyone. Point your client at `https://api.fereai.xyz/mcp` and sign in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where are my MCP wallets?">
    Each account you use through the MCP gets two wallets — one EVM and one Solana. Ask your AI *"What are my Fere wallet addresses?"* to see them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Points & Referrals

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I earn points?">
    Points come from your **on-chain trading volume** — 1 point per USD of trade value. They reset every Monday for the weekly leaderboard, and your all-time total is kept separately. See [Points & Leaderboard](/points).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a referral program?">
    A new one is on its way: Refer & Earn is becoming a **fee-share program** with multiple tiers. Details are coming in a few days — see [Refer & Earn](/refer-and-earn).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
