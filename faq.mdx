Everything you need to know about using Fere AI — from starting an agent to trading from your own AI.

## Agents

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a Fere agent?">
    An agent is an automated trader that runs one unique strategy. You pick an agent from the catalogue, add money to it from your Fere Wallet, and it trades on its own, day and night. See [Agents](/agents).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change an agent's strategy?">
    **No.** Each agent's strategy is built and maintained by Fere, and its logic can't be edited. Some agents let you adjust a few settings — like amount per trade, leverage, stop loss, or take profit — while others run with fixed settings.

    Want a custom agent or to run your own strategy? [Contact us](/support).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much do I need to start an agent?">
    It depends on the agent, and the **Add money** screen shows its exact minimum. Most agents need their amount per trade, often **$20** and never below $5. Perps agents that trade on Hyperliquid need at least **$50**. Fere suggests a few dollars more to cover fees. If an agent's minimum is out of reach, its page may suggest a smaller agent with a similar strategy. See [How much you need](/agents#how-much-you-need).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does it cost?">
    Fere charges **0.5% of each trade** (0.1% for perps agents), and no other fees. Some small costs come from the networks and markets rather than from Fere: converting deposits sent on networks other than Base, prices moving while a trade fills, and Hyperliquid's $1 fee when money comes back from it. See [Fees](/agents#fees).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I stop an agent?">
    Open the agent's page and choose **Stop**. It stops making new trades right away. **Stopping doesn't sell anything:** whatever it holds stays in its wallet, and any take-profit and stop-loss orders it placed stay on. Fere then offers to take its Cash back to your Fere Wallet. You can sell, take money out, or **Start again** whenever you like.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why hasn't my agent traded yet?">
    Agents only trade when their strategy signals an opportunity, so quiet stretches are normal — some strategies trade rarely. Settings change the size and exits of each trade, never how often the agent trades.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I lose money?">
    **Yes.** Agents trade real money in volatile markets, and past performance doesn't guarantee future results. Only fund an agent with money you can afford to lose.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Wallet & Funds

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is my money held?">
    In your **Fere Wallet**, and in the wallets of the agents you add money to. All of them are Coinbase CDP Server Wallets. Private keys live inside hardware-isolated Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs). An agent can only trade with its wallet, and only you can move money out of it. See [Wallet & Security](/wallet).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the Fere Wallet?">
    Your Fere Wallet is where your money lives on Fere. You deposit into it, add money from it to agents, and withdraw from it. Find it under **Portfolio → Fere Wallet**. See [Your Fere Wallet](/wallet#your-fere-wallet).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is Cash?">
    **Cash** is money that isn't in a trade, shown in US dollars. Behind the scenes it's USDC on the Base network, a digital dollar worth $1 each. Deposits land as Cash, and Cash is what you withdraw.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I don't own any crypto. How do I start?">
    Buy USDC on an exchange such as Coinbase, then send it to your Fere Wallet on the Base network. [New to Crypto](/new-to-crypto) walks you through it step by step.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I add money?">
    First deposit USDC into your Fere Wallet: **Portfolio → Fere Wallet → Deposit**, pick the network you're sending on, and send to the address shown. Then choose **Add money** on an agent's page. See [Deposit](/wallet#deposit).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which network should I send on?">
    **Base**, if your exchange or wallet supports it. Your Cash lives on Base, so a Base deposit arrives in full and usually within a minute. Other networks work too, but they're converted to Cash at a small cost, and amounts under $5 aren't converted. See [Deposit](/wallet#deposit).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does a deposit take?">
    Usually under a minute on Base, Solana, Arbitrum and BNB Chain, 1 to 2 minutes on Polygon, and 1 to 5 minutes on Ethereum. The deposit screen updates as soon as your money arrives.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I sent the wrong coin or used the wrong network. What now?">
    [Contact support](/support) right away with the transaction details. Only send the coin listed for the network you picked: anything else can be lost, and we can't always recover it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I withdraw?">
    Money leaves Fere only from your Fere Wallet. If it's in an agent, choose **Take money out** on the agent's page to send its Cash back to your Fere Wallet; sell or close what the agent holds first. Then go to **Portfolio → Fere Wallet → Withdraw**, enter an amount, and paste an address that can receive **USDC on Base**. See [Withdraw](/wallet#withdraw).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I have to pay tax?">
    Fere doesn't give tax advice. In many countries, each trade an agent makes can count as a taxable event. Your full trade history is in **Portfolio**. Check the rules where you live, or ask a tax professional.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is a token missing from my wallet?">
    It's most likely dust (worth under $2.00) or unverified (flagged as a scam, or very low liquidity). Both are hidden by default. Turn off **Hide dust** at the top of your holdings to see everything. See [Dust and unverified tokens](/wallet#dust-and-unverified-tokens).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why isn't a token counted in my balance?">
    If it has an **Unverified** tag, its price can't be trusted, so we leave it out of your totals and the agent's minimum, even though its quoted value appears on its row. If it's worth more than $2.00 you can try to sell it, but low-liquidity trades may not always go through. See [Dust and unverified tokens](/wallet#dust-and-unverified-tokens).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## MCP & Research

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where did Research go?">
    Research now runs through the **Fere MCP**. Connect it to Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Hermes, OpenClaw, or any other MCP client to research markets with Fere's tools. See [MCP Support](/mcp).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I trade through the MCP?">
    **Yes.** Through the MCP, your own AI can swap tokens, place limit orders, trade perps and prediction markets, and more — just ask it in plain language.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need approval to use the MCP?">
    **No.** The MCP is live and open to everyone. Point your client at `https://api.fereai.xyz/mcp` and sign in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where are my MCP wallets?">
    Each account you use through the MCP gets two wallets — one EVM and one Solana. Ask your AI *"What are my Fere wallet addresses?"* to see them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Points & Referrals

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I earn points?">
    Points come from your **on-chain trading volume** — 1 point per USD of trade value. They reset every Monday for the weekly leaderboard, and your all-time total is kept separately. See [Points & Leaderboard](/points).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a referral program?">
    A new one is on its way: Refer & Earn is becoming a **fee-share program** with multiple tiers. Details are coming in a few days — see [Refer & Earn](/refer-and-earn).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
