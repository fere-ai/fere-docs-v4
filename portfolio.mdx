The **Portfolio** page (`/portfolio`) is your book of accounts — Main plus every strategy wallet — with a combined net worth that does not change when you drill into a single wallet. The header chip left of notifications is that same combined total and opens this page.

## What You See on the Book

- **Total net worth** — Main + every strategy wallet (active and inactive). There is no separate “Balance” label on these totals.
- **Realized PnL** and **traded volume** — portfolio-level figures.
- **Allocation** — how value is split across Main, active strategies, and inactive wallets.
- **Wallets** — sticky heading; each row shows name, status, and USD.

The main list is **Main + wallets bound to an active strategy**. Those rows stay visible even at $0. Paused, stopped, or leftover wallets sit behind **Show inactive** even when they still hold funds. There is no separate Closed section.

Day one (nothing funded yet) shows a short **Deposit** / **Browse strategies** prompt above the wallets list. Main still appears so you can open it and deposit.

Strategy update emails and Telegram with an empty Main keep you here so you can deposit. After Main has a balance, Fere continues you into **Strategies** to configure and deploy that strategy. Dismissing a readiness prompt on a funded Main still leaves Portfolio as the book of accounts.

## Open a Wallet

Tap an account row:

- **Desktop** — in-page split: book on the left, selected wallet on the right (no overlay).
- **Mobile** — bottom sheet for the selected wallet.

### Main wallet

- Balance, primary **Add Funds**, and wallet details (EVM + Solana cluster → Your Wallets).
- Venue lens (All / Tokens / Polymarket / Hyperliquid / Yield) filters what is below.
- **Open Positions** — live holdings (sell / redeem where available).
- **History** — cross-venue activity; scrolls to load older pages (no “Load more”).

If Main has no open positions and no strategy fills yet, you see **No strategy trades yet** with **Go to strategies**. Positions and History are not stacked on top of each other.

### Strategy wallet

- Isolated addresses, **Fund** / **Withdraw** on the wallet header.
- Empty (unfunded) **active** strategies stay on the main rail at $0. Fund from Main to add capital.
- Empty holdings show **No holdings yet** and **Open strategy**. Fund lives on the header, not on the deposit-wallets row.
- **Positions** have **Sell** on every coin and position the strategy holds. It asks once — *"Sell {coin}? About $x goes to {strategy}'s wallet as USDC"* — then sells the whole position from that strategy's wallet, never from Main. Coins swap into the chain's stablecoin (a full sell also clears that coin's take-profit and cut-loss), Hyperliquid spot and perps go back to the strategy's Hyperliquid cash, and Polymarket shares sell at the live bid into its Polymarket cash. Cash rows have no Sell, and resolved Polymarket markets show **Redeem** instead. The sale shows in **Trades** once it settles.

## Fund / Deposit / Withdraw From the List

On each account row:

| Account | Actions |
| --- | --- |
| Main | **Deposit** → same Add Funds flow as elsewhere |
| Strategy | **Fund** / **Withdraw** → strategy transfer modals |

These open the modal directly. After a successful fund, withdraw, or sell, **every wallet in the book refreshes** (Main + strategies) so moved value is not stale. One wallet failing to refresh does not fail the transfer itself.

## Hide Dust & Unverified

The same hide-dust / hide-unverified toggles used in the wallet panel apply here. If filters zero every visible balance, the empty prompt may appear — but the **wallets list stays** so you can still open Main for wallets and history.
