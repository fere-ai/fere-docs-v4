Portfolio is where you see everything you hold and everything your strategies have done — one summary, broken down by account.

## The Portfolio Page

Click **Portfolio** in the top navigation bar. You'll land on a master–detail view:

- **Accounts rail** — A list of every account you can view: your **Main Wallet** and a row for each deployed strategy that trades with its own funds.
- **Summary card** — For the selected account: total value, live (unrealized) P&L, realized P&L, and total traded volume.
- **Allocation bar** — A visual breakdown of your total value by wallet or venue.
- **Positions and holdings** — Open positions and token balances for the selected account.
- **History** — A table of past trades and transfers for the selected account. For an agent's account it also lists cash deposits, conversions, and withdrawals (filter with **Cash**).

<Info icon="chart-pie">
  **Numbers can still be settling.** If a deposit or withdrawal is still confirming on-chain, the summary shows a loading state for the affected figures rather than a number that would immediately be wrong.
</Info>

**Rail order and stopped accounts** — Your Main Wallet stays at the top. Strategy accounts below it are sorted by balance, highest first; when balances tie, running strategies come before ones waiting for money. A stopped strategy that still holds funds stays in the list. Stopped strategies that are empty or hold only a few cents of dust are tucked behind **Show stopped & empty** at the bottom of the rail — click it to expand (it then reads **Hide stopped & empty**).

For a strategy account, the positions list has a **Hide dust** toggle that hides tiny balances, the same one used in the wallet panel.

## Main Wallet vs. Strategy Accounts

Your **Main Wallet** is the account you fund directly and trade from in chat. Each **strategy account** is a separate, dedicated wallet Fere creates when you deploy a [strategy](/strategies) that trades with its own funds — its Portfolio row shows only that strategy's positions, trades, and P&L, kept apart from your main balance.

Selecting a strategy account here shows the same holdings, positions, and trade history you'd find on that [strategy's detail page](/strategies#strategy-detail-page), filtered to just that account.

## Adding and Moving Funds

From Portfolio you can:

- **Add funds** — Deposit into your Main Wallet by transfer or on-chain address.
- **Fund a strategy account** — Move funds from your Main Wallet into a strategy account so it has capital to trade with.
- **Withdraw from a strategy account** — Pull funds back out of a strategy account to your Main Wallet. For an agent, sell positions to Cash and use **Withdraw** on the Cash row; see [Cash, Selling, and Withdrawing](/agents#cash-selling-and-withdrawing).

<Tip>
  **First time here?** If you haven't funded any account yet, Portfolio shows a getting-started view instead of an empty table — use it to add funds or deploy your first strategy.
</Tip>

## Signed Out?

You can preview the Portfolio layout signed out, but real balances, positions, and history require a connected Fere wallet — sign in to see your own numbers.
