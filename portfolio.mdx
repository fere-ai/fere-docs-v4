Open **Portfolio** from the top navigation to see everything Fere is holding on your behalf — your Main wallet, plus a dedicated wallet for every strategy you've deployed.

## Accounts, Side by Side

Portfolio uses a two-pane layout:

- **Left — Accounts** — Your **Main** wallet, followed by one card per active or archived [strategy](/strategies). Each card shows that account's balance at a glance.
- **Right — Detail** — Whichever account you've selected: its holdings, PnL, and actions.

<Tip>
  Every deployed strategy gets its own wallet, separate from your Main wallet. This keeps a strategy's capital and activity easy to track independently of the rest of your funds.
</Tip>

## Main Wallet

Select **Main** to see:

- **Holdings by venue** — Assets grouped by chain (EVM chains and Solana) plus any [Hyperliquid](/hyperliquid) perp collateral and positions.
- **A dust/unverified-balance filter** — Hide small or unverified balances to keep the list focused on holdings that matter.
- **Limit Orders** — All your active limit orders in one place.
- **TP/SL Orders** — Active take-profit and stop-loss positions appear under the relevant token.
- **Add Funds** — Deposit via an EVM transfer, a Solana transfer, or another method — pick your chain and token and Fere shows you the deposit address.
- **Your Wallets** — Tap the wallet cluster icon to view your EVM and Solana addresses, copy them, open them in a block explorer, or reveal your private key. See [Wallet](/wallet) for the full key-export flow.

## Strategy Wallets

Select any strategy card to see that strategy's own holdings, separate from Main, along with **Fund** and **Withdraw** actions scoped to that wallet.

## Portfolio Summary

Above the accounts list, a summary bar totals your position across every wallet:

- **Total value** — Combined balance across Main and all strategy wallets.
- **PnL** — Live (unrealized) and realized profit/loss.
- **Traded volume** — Cumulative volume across your accounts.
- **Allocation** — A bar showing how your value is split across venues.

## Transaction History

A history table lists deposits, withdrawals, swaps, and trades across the selected account, so you can review exactly what happened and when.

<Note>
  New to Fere and haven't funded a wallet yet? Portfolio shows a simple prompt to deposit or [browse strategies](/strategies) instead of an empty table.
</Note>
