You don't need to know crypto to use Fere. You need one thing: **USDC**, a digital dollar. This page shows you how to get it, send it to Fere, and start your first agent.

## The Two Things to Know

- **USDC is a digital dollar.** One USDC is worth one US dollar. It's what you send to Fere, and it shows in the app as **Cash**, in US dollars.
- **A network is the road your money travels on.** The same USDC can travel on different networks (Base, Solana, Ethereum, and others). When you send, the network you pick in your exchange must match the network you pick in Fere. **Base** is the simplest choice for Fere.

## Your First Deposit, Step by Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for Fere">
    Create an account at [fereai.xyz](https://www.fereai.xyz/agents). Your **Fere Wallet** is created for you. There's nothing to connect or install.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get USDC on an exchange">
    If you don't own any crypto, buy USDC on an exchange such as Coinbase. If you hold another coin, like Bitcoin, swap it for USDC in the exchange first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your Fere Wallet address">
    In Fere, open **Portfolio → Fere Wallet → Deposit** and pick **Base**. Copy the address shown, or scan its QR code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the USDC">
    In your exchange, choose to send (or withdraw) USDC, paste your Fere Wallet address, and **pick Base as the network**. Check that the first and last 4 characters of the address match, then confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait about a minute">
    On Base it usually arrives in under a minute. The deposit screen updates, and the amount shows as Cash in your Fere Wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start an agent">
    Open **Agents**, pick one whose strategy you like, and choose **Add money**. The money moves from your Fere Wallet to the agent, and it starts trading once it has its minimum. See [Agents](/agents).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Send only USDC, and only on the network you picked.** Crypto transfers can't be reversed. Sending a different coin, or picking a different network in your exchange than in Fere, can lose the money. If something goes wrong, [contact support](/support) right away.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  **Not sure? Send a small amount first.** Send a few dollars, wait for it to show up as Cash, then send the rest.
</Tip>

## Getting Your Money Back

1. On the agent's page, choose **Take money out**. Its Cash goes back to your Fere Wallet. If the agent holds coins, sell them to Cash first.
2. In **Portfolio → Fere Wallet**, choose **Withdraw**, enter an amount, and paste the address to send to. It's sent as **USDC on Base**, so in your exchange copy your USDC deposit address with **Base** selected.

See [Withdraw](/wallet#withdraw) for details.

## Words You'll See

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Cash** | Money in Fere that isn't in a trade, shown in US dollars. It's USDC on Base. |
| **Fere Wallet** | Your account's wallet on Fere. Money comes in and goes out through it. |
| **Agent** | An automated trader that follows one fixed strategy with the money you add to it. |
| **Network** | The blockchain your money travels on, such as Base or Solana. Sender and receiver must use the same one. |
| **Address** | A long string of letters and numbers that identifies a wallet on a network, like an account number. |
| **Token / coin** | A crypto asset an agent can buy, such as ETH, BTC, or a memecoin. |
| **Memecoin** | A token driven mostly by hype and social media. Prices can swing very fast, up or down. |
| **Take profit** | A price at which the agent sells to lock in a gain. |
| **Stop loss** | A price at which the agent sells to limit a loss. |
| **Perps** | Perpetual futures: bets on a coin's price going up or down, without owning the coin. |
| **Leverage** | Trading with more than you put in. 5× leverage makes gains and losses 5 times bigger, and a large enough move against the trade can wipe out the money in it (called **liquidation**). |
| **Slippage** | The difference between the price you were quoted and the price the trade actually fills at. |
| **Dust** | Leftover tokens worth under $2. Hidden by default. See [Dust and unverified tokens](/wallet#dust-and-unverified-tokens). |
| **Backtest** | A replay of a strategy on past prices. It shows how the rules would have done, not what will happen. See [How backtests work](/agents#how-backtests-work). |

## Before You Start

- **You can lose money.** Agents trade real money in volatile markets. Only add money you can afford to lose.
- **Taxes.** In many countries, each trade can count as a taxable event. Fere doesn't give tax advice; your full trade history is in **Portfolio**.
- **Questions?** Check the [FAQ](/faq) or [contact support](/support).
