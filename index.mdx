## How Agents Work

1. **Pick an agent** — every agent page shows what it trades, how it exits, and how it has performed.
2. **Fund its wallet** — each agent gets its own wallet, secured by Coinbase. Its balance shows as Cash, in US dollars.
3. **It trades, day and night** — follow every trade from the agent's page or your Portfolio. Stop it or withdraw whenever you like.

Fees are simple: **0.5% of each trade** (0.1% for perps agents), and nothing else.
