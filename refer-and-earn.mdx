Refer & Earn is live. Invite friends to Fere and get **20–50% of the fees** Fere collects from them. There is no limit on how many people you can invite. Rewards start when they trade, and they are paid to your **Cash** every Monday.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Earn">
    Choose **Earn** in the top navigation on a computer, or the **Earn** tab at the bottom on a phone. Sign in if you haven't. Copy your invite link from that page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Friends fund and trade">
    Rewards start when the people you invite trade. Signing up alone does not pay anything yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You get 20–50% of their fees">
    The share depends on your tier. It is paid to your Cash every Monday. The Earn page shows your tier, your earnings, and the next tier.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Tier | Your share | How you reach it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Base** | 20% | Anyone |
| **Silver** | 30% | 5 or more active people you invited, and $25,000 or more of volume |
| **Gold** | 40% | 20 or more active people you invited, and $250,000 or more of volume |
| **Partner** | 50% | Invite only |

Tiers are re-checked monthly, using the last 30 days. Partner is a custom deal.

<Info>
  Fere's fee is **0.5% of each trade** (0.1% for perps agents). See [Fees](/agents#fees). Someone who joins with your invite code gets **10% of their own fees** back. They can add the code in Settings before their first deposit.
</Info>
