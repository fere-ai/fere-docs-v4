Refer & Earn is live. Invite friends to Fere and get a **share of the fees Fere collects** from everyone you invite.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Earn">
    Choose **Earn** in the top navigation on a computer, or the **Earn** tab at the bottom on a phone. Sign in if you haven't.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your invite link">
    Your personal invite link appears once you're signed in. Share it with friends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn from their fees">
    When the people you invite trade on Fere, you get a share of the fees Fere collects from them. Track it on the Earn page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Fere's fee is **0.5% of each trade** (0.1% for perps agents). See [Fees](/agents#fees). Your share and current earnings are shown on the Earn page.
</Info>
