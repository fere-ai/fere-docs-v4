Earn is Fere's fee-share program. Share your invite link, and when the people you invite fund and trade, you get **20–50% of the fees Fere collects from them**, paid to your Cash every Monday.

## Where to Find Earn

- **Fere app (iPhone):** tap the **Earn** tab.
- **Web on a phone:** tap the **Earn** tab at the bottom, or open your account menu and choose **Earn**.
- **Web on a computer:** click **Earn** in the top navigation.

You need to be signed in. If you're not, Earn asks you to **Sign in** to get your invite link.

<Note>
  Earn replaces the old Points page and the Refer & Earn pop-up. Old links to them now open Earn.
</Note>

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share your link">
    Copy your invite link or code from Earn and send it to anyone. There's no limit on invites.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Friends fund and trade">
    Rewards start when the people you invite trade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You get 20–50% of their fees">
    Your share is worked out every Monday and paid to your Cash the same day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your Invite Link and Code

At the top of Earn you'll find **Your invite link**, which looks like `fereai.xyz/r/K7M2QX`, and your **Code** on its own (`K7M2QX` in this example).

- On a computer, use **Copy link**. On a phone, use **Share link** to send it from your share sheet. You can also copy just the code.
- Invite as many people as you like. **Your code never expires.**
- When someone opens your link, their browser remembers your code for 30 days. If they sign up on that browser within that time, your code is added to their account automatically, so they don't need to type it.
- Someone can also type your code in by hand (see [Adding an Invite Code](#adding-an-invite-code)).

## Tiers

Your tier sets how much of your referees' fees you get. Everyone starts on Base.

| Tier | Your share of fees | What it takes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Base** | 20% | Anyone |
| **Silver** | 30% | 5+ active referees and $25k+ referred volume in the last 30 days |
| **Gold** | 40% | 20+ active referees and $250k+ referred volume in the last 30 days |
| **Partner** | 50% | Invite only |

- **Active** means a person you invited has funded $100 or more and traded in the last 30 days.
- Tiers are **re-checked monthly**, based on the last 30 days.
- The **Your tier** card shows your current tier and share, plus two progress bars toward the next tier: **Active referees** and **30-day volume**. Tap **All tiers** to see the full table.
- On Gold, the card tells you you're on the top tier. Partner is a custom deal, so tiers and progress bars don't apply.

## What You'll See

Earn changes as your invites start working.

### No invites yet

Until someone uses your code, Earn shows your invite link, your tier, and the three **How it works** steps.

### Invited, not earning yet

Once people sign up with your code, you'll see four numbers:

- **Signed up**: how many people joined with your code. This one updates right away.
- **Active · 30d**, **Referred volume**, and **Fee share earned**: these show a dash (—) until your first weekly update.

A line under the numbers tells you when they'll arrive, for example: *"First numbers land Mon 23 Nov. Fee share is paid to your Cash the same day."*

### Earning

After your first weekly update, all four numbers fill in. **Fee share earned** is your total paid so far. The line underneath shows when the numbers last changed and when they change next, for example: *"Updated Mondays · last Mon 16 Nov · next Mon 23 Nov. Fee share is paid to your Cash the same day."*

## Weekly Payouts

Everything on Earn except **Signed up** updates **once a week, on Mondays**. On the same day, what you earned is paid to your **Cash**.

The **next update** date is always the coming Monday. If it's Monday and that day's update hasn't landed yet, it shows today.

## Paid to Cash

Once you've been paid, a **Paid to Cash** list shows your most recent Monday deposits, newest first. Each one shows the date, the amount, and what it was for:

- **Fee share · 30%**: your share of fees from people you invited, at your tier's rate that week.
- **Cashback · 50% of your fees**: fees we gave back to you.
- **Invite bonus · 10% of your fees**: your own fees back for joining with an invite code.

## Cashback

Some weeks we give back part or all of the trading fees you pay. That's **cashback**, and it lands in your Cash on Monday.

The **Cashback** card shows your total cashback so far and, after your first one, the latest week, for example: *"50% of your fees back for the week of 9 Nov"*.

## Adding an Invite Code

If someone invited you, add their code to get **10% of the fees you pay back in your Cash every Monday**.

On Earn, look for **Got an invite code?**, tap **Enter code**, type the code, then tap **Apply code**. You can also add it from **Settings**.

The rules:

- **Only before your first deposit.** Once you've funded your account, you can't add a code, and the **Got an invite code?** card disappears.
- **One code per account.** Once a code is added, it can't be changed.
- **Not your own code.** Use the code of the person who invited you.

When the code is added you'll see *"Invite code added. 10% of your fees come back to you every Monday."* An **Invite bonus** card then appears on Earn, showing the code you joined with, your total so far, and either the latest week or the date your first payout lands.

### If your code doesn't work

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| *That code doesn't exist. Check it and try again.* | The code was typed wrong or isn't a Fere code. |
| *That's your own code. Use the code of the person who invited you.* | You entered your own invite code. |
| *You've already added an invite code.* | Your account already has a code. Each account can have one. |
| *This invite code is no longer active.* | The code can't be used any more. Ask for another one. |
| *Invite codes can only be added before your first deposit.* | You've already funded your account. |
| *Too many invite code attempts. Try again later.* | Too many tries in a short time. Wait, then try again. If the wait is short, it tells you how many seconds. |
| *Could not apply the invite code. Try again.* | Something went wrong, often the connection. Try again. |

## If Earn Doesn't Load

If you see **Couldn't load your earnings** or **Couldn't load Earn**, tap **Retry**. Your earnings are safe; only the page failed to load.

## Past Points

Points are no longer earned. If you earned points under the old program, Earn shows them as **Past platform points** at the bottom of the page.
