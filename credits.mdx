Every action Fere takes on your behalf costs credits. Think of credits as the fuel that powers your AI agent — you get a free signup bonus, and can get more when you need them.

## How Credits Work

Credits are the currency of the Fere platform. Chat queries and most scheduled actions use dynamic pricing based on complexity.

| Action | Credit Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Fere AI Agent Query | Dynamic per query (based on complexity) |

## Getting More Credits

There is no longer a Pricing page or in-app credit top-up in the web app, and the profile menu no longer shows a credit balance or an **Add Credits** button. If you run low, Fere shows a **Not enough credits** prompt when an action needs more than your balance. For help with your balance, reach out through [Support](/support).
