Fere sends product messages by email when the account has an email address and you did not sign in with a wallet.

Telegram is used in two cases, after you link the Fere bot:

- The account has no email address.
- You signed in with a wallet.

A wallet sign-in account receives the Telegram message even when an email address is also on the account.

## Link Telegram

Open the Fere bot in Telegram and send `/start`. That links the chat to your Fere account. Later messages arrive in that chat.

## What the message contains

Each message is text. It can include one button. The button has a label and a link. The text and the button are the contents of that send.

A message with no link arrives as text only.

One send arrives once. A later send arrives on its own.
