Every agent you start trades from its **own wallet**. Under the hood, each one is powered by Coinbase's most advanced wallet infrastructure.

## Agent Wallets (Fere App)

In the Fere app, your money lives in the wallets of the agents you start — one wallet per agent. There's no separate personal wallet to manage.

- **Cash** — money waiting to be traded, shown in US dollars.
- **Holdings** — whatever the agent currently holds from its trades.
- **Activity** — every trade, deposit, and withdrawal for that wallet.

You can see each agent's wallet on its page, and all of them together under **Portfolio** in the top navigation.

### If holdings say temporarily unavailable

Balances come from live on-chain providers. When those providers are slow or overloaded, Portfolio and agent wallets show **Holdings temporarily unavailable** (or **Temporarily unavailable** on the compact balance row) instead of inventing a $0 balance.

- Your funds are not moved or lost.
- The app does **not** keep hammering a failing holdings request in the background.
- Use **Retry** on the holdings panel, refresh holdings from Portfolio, or leave the page and come back when the network is quieter.

### Add funds

Use **Fund** (or **Fund more**) on an agent's page. The funding screen shows the networks you can send from, the address to send to on each, and the agent's minimum. Deposits land as Cash.

### Withdraw

Choose **Withdraw** on the agent's page, enter an amount in USD, and the address to send it to. If the agent is holding positions rather than Cash, sell them back to Cash first. You can withdraw whether the agent is running or stopped.

### Dust and unverified tokens

To keep your wallet easy to read, Fere hides two kinds of tokens by default. They're still in your wallet. Nothing is moved or deleted.

- **Dust**: any token worth less than **$2.00**. Small leftovers from trades and rounding build up over time, so we hide them.
- **Unverified**: a token flagged as a likely scam, or one with less than **$10,000** of trading liquidity. Anyone can send any token to any wallet, so unverified tokens are often "airdrops" you never asked for. Their price is also unreliable: a thin or fake market can show a value you could never actually sell for.

**To see them**, turn off **Hide dust** at the top of the holdings list in your wallet or in **Portfolio**. Unverified tokens then appear with an **Unverified** tag next to their quoted value.

**What this means for your balance:**

- Unverified tokens are **never counted** in your totals or toward an agent's minimum, even when they're visible. The value on their row is a quote, not money you can count on.
- While **Hide dust** is on, your total only includes what you see, so tiny dust amounts are left out. Turn it off to include them.
- If an unverified token is worth more than **$2.00**, you can try to **Sell** it from the agent's page. Because these tokens have little liquidity, the trade may not always go through, and when it does you may get less than the quoted value. Some scam tokens can't be sold at all.

<Warning>
  If a token you don't recognize shows up in your wallet, don't interact with it, and never visit a website named in a token's name or description. These are common scam tactics.
</Warning>

## MCP Wallets

If you trade through the [Fere MCP](/mcp) from your own AI client, each account you create gets **two wallets**: one **EVM** wallet and one **Solana** wallet. To see their addresses or balances, just ask your AI — for example, *"What are my Fere wallet addresses?"*

## What Makes This Secure

- **Keys never exposed** — Private keys are generated and stored inside hardware-isolated enclaves.
- **Agents can only trade** — An agent can trade with its wallet, but only you can withdraw from it.
- **Policy enforcement** — Transaction rules are enforced at the enclave level.
- **Fast signing** — Wallet creation takes under 500ms. Transaction signing under 200ms.

**Infrastructure:** Coinbase CDP · Trusted Execution (TEE) · AWS Nitro Enclaves
