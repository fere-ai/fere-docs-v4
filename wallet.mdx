Your wallet shows balances, assets, and active orders in real time from the **Portfolio** page. Under the hood, it's powered by Coinbase's most advanced wallet infrastructure.

## What You See

Open **Portfolio** from the top navigation, then select **Main wallet** (pinned first in the Accounts rail — this is your parent Fere wallet, as opposed to a strategy's own dedicated wallet). From there you can see:

- **Total balance** — Your aggregate holdings across all chains, with a composition donut and a venue lens (Tokens / Polymarket / Hyperliquid) to filter what's below it.
- **Your Wallets** — Click the wallet-cluster icon next to your balance to open a modal listing every wallet address (EVM and Solana), each with copy, block-explorer, and reveal-private-key controls.
- **Add Funds** — Opens the deposit flow, which also lists every chain Fere currently supports.
- **Positions / Trades / Orders** — A segmented control switches the view below between your current holdings (with a "Hide dust" filter for tiny balances), your trade history, and your active limit orders. Take-profit and stop-loss orders attached to a position are managed from that position's row under **Positions**.

<Info icon="lock">
  **Powered by Coinbase CDP Server Wallets.** Every Fere wallet is a Coinbase Developer Platform Server Wallet — the same infrastructure trusted by the world's largest crypto exchange. Private keys live inside Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs) and never leave the secure enclave.
</Info>

## You Own Your Wallet

This is critical: **your wallet is fully owned by you.** In **Your Wallets** (Portfolio → wallet-cluster icon), each wallet card has a reveal-private-key control. Click it to reveal and export your private key. You can then import that key into any external wallet like MetaMask or Phantom — meaning your funds are never locked inside Fere.

<Check>
  **Full Key Ownership.** Your private key is yours. Export it anytime and use it in MetaMask (for EVM chains) or Phantom (for Solana). Fere is non-custodial — you can always access your funds independently. See the [FAQ](/faq) for step-by-step export instructions.
</Check>

## What Makes This Secure

- **One wallet, every chain** — A single wallet identity works across all supported EVM chains and Solana.
- **Keys never exposed** — Private keys are generated and stored inside hardware-isolated enclaves. Not even Coinbase or Fere can access them.
- **Policy enforcement** — Transaction rules like spend limits and address allowlists are enforced at the enclave level.
- **Sub-200ms signing** — Wallet creation takes under 500ms. Transaction signing under 200ms.
- **Non-custodial** — Fere cannot seize, freeze, or move your funds. Period.

**Infrastructure:** Coinbase CDP · Trusted Execution (TEE) · AWS Nitro Enclaves
