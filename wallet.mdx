Your wallet lives on the **Portfolio** page — showing your balance, holdings, and trade history in real time. Under the hood, it's powered by Coinbase's most advanced wallet infrastructure.

## What You See

Open **Portfolio** from the navigation to reach your wallet. At a glance, you can see:

- **Total Net Worth** — Your aggregate holdings across all chains and accounts.
- **Wallet cluster button** — A stacked-avatar button (one icon per wallet kind — EVM, Solana) sits next to your balance and opens the **Your Wallets** modal, which lists every wallet address with copy, block-explorer, and reveal-private-key controls.
- **Add Funds** — One-click deposit into your wallet. The deposit view also lists every chain Fere currently supports.
- **Open Positions** — Your holdings, with a **venue lens** to filter by **Tokens**, **Polymarket**, or **Hyperliquid**. **Hide dust** and **Hide unverified** checkboxes filter out tiny and unverified balances.
- **Trade History** — Every trade and transfer, newest first, with the activity that originated it.

Specialized balances also appear here when you use integrated products:

- **Polymarket** — pUSD cash and open prediction-market positions. See [Polymarket](/polymarket).
- **Hyperliquid** — **USDC (Hyperliquid)** collateral and open perp positions. Use **Sell** on USDC to withdraw, or on a perp row to close. See [Hyperliquid](/hyperliquid).
- **Fere Earn (Yield)** — Existing balances remain visible, but the dedicated Yield page and its **Manage** action are currently unavailable. Ask Fere in chat to review or manage an existing position.

<Info icon="lock">
  **Powered by Coinbase CDP Server Wallets.** Every Fere wallet is a Coinbase Developer Platform Server Wallet — the same infrastructure trusted by the world's largest crypto exchange. Private keys live inside Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs) and never leave the secure enclave.
</Info>

## You Own Your Wallet

This is critical: **your wallet is fully owned by you.** On **Portfolio**, click the wallet cluster button next to your balance to bring up **Your Wallets**, then use the reveal-key control on any wallet card to export your private key. You can then import that key into any external wallet like MetaMask or Phantom — meaning your funds are never locked inside Fere.

<Check>
  **Full Key Ownership.** Your private key is yours. Export it anytime and use it in MetaMask (for EVM chains) or Phantom (for Solana). Fere is non-custodial — you can always access your funds independently. See the [FAQ](/faq) for step-by-step export instructions.
</Check>

## What Makes This Secure

- **One wallet, every chain** — A single wallet identity works across all supported EVM chains and Solana.
- **Keys never exposed** — Private keys are generated and stored inside hardware-isolated enclaves. Not even Coinbase or Fere can access them.
- **Policy enforcement** — Transaction rules like spend limits and address allowlists are enforced at the enclave level.
- **Sub-200ms signing** — Wallet creation takes under 500ms. Transaction signing under 200ms.
- **Non-custodial** — Fere cannot seize, freeze, or move your funds. Period.

## Strategy Wallets

Farming strategies (Polymarket Farming) and other on-chain scheduled strategies each trade from their **own dedicated wallet** — a separate address from your main wallet. The **Accounts** rail on [Portfolio](/portfolio) lists your main wallet plus a row per strategy wallet; selecting one shows its balance and **Fund** / **Withdraw** buttons.

<Warning>
  **Pausing or stopping a strategy does not return its funds.** The strategy wallet and any open positions stay in place until you withdraw manually.
</Warning>

If a strategy is cancelled while its wallet still holds a balance, it moves to a **Closed · still funded** group in the Accounts rail — Withdraw is still available there, and the row disappears once the balance reaches zero.

See [Strategies → Strategy Wallets](/strategies#strategy-wallets) for the full setup, funding, and withdrawal flow.

## Transaction Activity

Every swap, transfer, bridge, limit order, TP/SL execution, Polymarket order, and Hyperliquid action is logged automatically. Click the **notification bell** in the top bar to see your most recent activity, or click the expand icon to open the full history at **Activities**.

- **Filter by date** — All, Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, or a custom date range.
- **Explorer links** — Cards with an on-chain transaction show a link to view it on the relevant block explorer.
- **Jump back to context** — Activity tied to a chat or strategy links directly back to it.

<Tip>
  For a portfolio-wide view that pairs this activity feed with your total value and PnL, see [Portfolio](/portfolio).
</Tip>
