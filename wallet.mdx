Your money lives in one place: your **Fere Wallet**. You deposit into it, add money from it to the agents you start, and withdraw from it. Every wallet, including each agent's, is powered by Coinbase's most advanced wallet infrastructure.

## How Money Moves

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deposit into your Fere Wallet">
    Send USDC to your Fere Wallet from an exchange or another wallet. It arrives as **Cash**, shown in US dollars.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add money to an agent">
    On an agent's page, choose **Add money**. The amount moves from your Fere Wallet to the agent's own wallet, and the agent trades with it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take money out of an agent">
    **Take money out** sends an agent's Cash back to your Fere Wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw from your Fere Wallet">
    **Withdraw** sends Cash from your Fere Wallet to an address you paste. This is the only way money leaves Fere.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your Fere Wallet

Open **Portfolio** in the top navigation and choose **Fere Wallet**. You'll see its **Cash** and two buttons: **Deposit** and **Withdraw**.

- **Cash** — money that isn't in a trade, shown in US dollars. Behind the scenes it's USDC on the Base network, a digital dollar worth $1 each.
- **Your Fere Wallet address** is the only address you ever send money to. Agents never show a deposit address of their own.

### Deposit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Deposit">
    In **Portfolio → Fere Wallet**, choose **Deposit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the network you're sending on">
    Pick the network your exchange or wallet will send on. The address shown is your Fere Wallet on that network, with a QR code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send USDC to the address">
    Copy the address, paste it into your exchange or wallet, and send. Check that the first and last 4 characters match before you confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for it to land">
    The screen updates as soon as your money arrives, and it shows as Cash.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Network | Coin to send | Usually arrives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Base** (recommended) | USDC | Under a minute |
| Solana | USDC | Under a minute |
| Arbitrum | USDC | Under a minute |
| BNB Chain | USDC | Under a minute |
| Polygon | USDC | 1 to 2 minutes |
| Ethereum | USDC | 1 to 5 minutes |
| Robinhood Chain | USDG | Under a minute |

<Tip>
  **On Coinbase? Send USDC on Base.** Base is the network your Cash lives on, so a Base deposit arrives in full with nothing to convert. Only have Bitcoin or another coin? Swap it for USDC in your exchange first.
</Tip>

**Deposits on other networks.** USDC sent on any network other than Base is converted to Cash automatically. The conversion has a small cost, so slightly less than you sent arrives as Cash. Send at least **$5** on these networks; smaller amounts aren't converted.

<Warning>
  **Only send the coin listed for the network you picked.** Sending a different coin, or sending on a different network than the one you picked, can lose the money. If it happens, [contact support](/support) right away.
</Warning>

### Withdraw

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Withdraw">
    In **Portfolio → Fere Wallet**, choose **Withdraw**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount and address">
    Enter an amount in USD and paste the address to send it to. Withdrawals are sent as **USDC on Base**, so use an address that can receive USDC on Base, such as your Coinbase USDC address with Base selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the address and send">
    Fere shows the address with its first and last 4 characters highlighted. Check they match where you're sending it, then confirm. A wrong address can't be undone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only Cash can be withdrawn. If your money is in an agent, take it out of the agent first.

## Agent Wallets

Every agent you start trades from its **own wallet**. The agent can only trade with it, and only you can move money out of it.

- **Cash** — money the agent hasn't put into a trade yet.
- **Holdings** — whatever the agent currently holds from its trades.
- **Activity** — every trade and money movement for that wallet.

You can see each agent's wallet on its page, and all of them together under **Portfolio**.

### Add money

On the agent's page, choose **Add money** and enter a whole-dollar amount. It moves from your Fere Wallet Cash to the agent. If your Fere Wallet doesn't have enough Cash, Fere shows your deposit address first, then lets you add the money once your deposit lands.

### Take money out

On the agent's page, choose **Take money out**. The agent's Cash goes back to your Fere Wallet. It can't be sent anywhere else. From there, you can [withdraw](#withdraw) it.

Only Cash can be taken out. If the agent is holding coins or open positions, sell or close them first. You can take money out whether the agent is running or stopped.

**Agents that trade on Hyperliquid.** Money on Hyperliquid comes back to your Fere Wallet as USDC on Arbitrum and is converted to Cash. Hyperliquid charges a **$1** fee, so the smallest amount you can take out is **$6**.

### Dust and unverified tokens

To keep your wallet easy to read, Fere hides two kinds of tokens by default. They're still in your wallet. Nothing is moved or deleted.

- **Dust**: any token worth less than **$2.00**. Small leftovers from trades and rounding build up over time, so we hide them.
- **Unverified**: a token flagged as a likely scam, or one with less than **$10,000** of trading liquidity. Anyone can send any token to any wallet, so unverified tokens are often "airdrops" you never asked for. Their price is also unreliable: a thin or fake market can show a value you could never actually sell for.

**To see them**, turn off **Hide dust** at the top of the holdings list in your wallet or in **Portfolio**. Unverified tokens then appear with an **Unverified** tag next to their quoted value.

**What this means for your balance:**

- Unverified tokens are **never counted** in your totals or toward an agent's minimum, even when they're visible. The value on their row is a quote, not money you can count on.
- While **Hide dust** is on, your total only includes what you see, so tiny dust amounts are left out. Turn it off to include them.
- If an unverified token is worth more than **$2.00**, you can try to **Sell** it from the agent's page. Because these tokens have little liquidity, the trade may not always go through, and when it does you may get less than the quoted value. Some scam tokens can't be sold at all.

<Warning>
  If a token you don't recognize shows up in your wallet, don't interact with it, and never visit a website named in a token's name or description. These are common scam tactics.
</Warning>

## MCP Wallets

If you trade through the [Fere MCP](/mcp) from your own AI client, each account you create gets **two wallets**: one **EVM** wallet and one **Solana** wallet. To see their addresses or balances, just ask your AI — for example, *"What are my Fere wallet addresses?"*

## What Makes This Secure

- **Keys never exposed** — Private keys are generated and stored inside hardware-isolated enclaves.
- **Agents can only trade** — An agent can trade with its wallet, but only you can move money out of it, and only back to your Fere Wallet.
- **Policy enforcement** — Transaction rules are enforced at the enclave level.
- **Fast signing** — Wallet creation takes under 500ms. Transaction signing under 200ms.

**Infrastructure:** Coinbase CDP · Trusted Execution (TEE) · AWS Nitro Enclaves
