Fere AI keeps a running feed of everything that happens on your account — completed trades, transfers, and account alerts — so you never have to wonder whether an action went through.

## How It Works

The **bell icon** in the top bar shows your recent activity, with a badge for anything unread. Click it for a quick dropdown, or open the full **Activities** page for your complete history.

## What You'll See

- **Transaction activity** — swaps, bridges, transfers, and limit order updates, along with their outcome.
- **Alerts** — price triggers and similar account alerts.
- **System messages** — account-level updates from Fere AI.

## Filtering Your History

On the Activities page, use the time range tabs to narrow what you see — **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 Days**, **Last 30 Days**, or a custom date range. Results are grouped by day (Today, Yesterday, Older) so recent events are easy to scan.

<Tip>
  Unread activities are marked so they stand out. Viewing one — or waiting about an hour — automatically marks it read.
</Tip>
