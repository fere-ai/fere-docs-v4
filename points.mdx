Points reward your on-chain trading volume — the more volume you trade, the more you earn. Climb the leaderboard and build your streak. Points refresh every Monday.

## How Points Work

Points are earned from one thing: **on-chain trading volume**. Open **Points** from the top navigation to see your stats.

| Activity | Points Earned |
| --- | --- |
| On-chain trading volume | 1 point per USD of trade value |

<Tip>
  **Retired activities.** Activities that used to earn points, like chat, no longer do. Points you already earned from them are preserved.
</Tip>

## What You See

The Points dashboard gives you a clear snapshot of your standing:

- **Total** — Your all-time accumulated points.
- **Last Week** — Points earned in the previous weekly cycle.
- **Rank** — Your current position among all users (e.g., Top 10%).
- **Streak** — How many consecutive weeks you've been active, shown as a progress bar.

<Info>
  **Weekly Refresh.** Points reset every Monday. This keeps the leaderboard competitive and gives everyone a fresh chance to climb the ranks each week. Your all-time total is tracked separately, so your lifetime contribution is never lost.
</Info>

## Points Breakdown

On the Points page, a **Points Breakdown** section shows exactly where your points came from as a bar chart. Categories with zero points are hidden automatically. Click **"How to Earn Points"** for the full breakdown.

## Leaderboard

Below the breakdown, the **Leaderboard** shows the top 30 performers for the current week. Your rank, username (partially masked for privacy), and point total are displayed. If you're in the top positions, you'll see gold, silver, and bronze medals next to your rank.

<Tip>
  **Why Points Matter.** Points are more than just a vanity metric. Users who consistently rank high on the leaderboard are building a track record of platform activity — and that will matter as Fere introduces new reward tiers, airdrops, and exclusive features for top contributors. Start building your streak now.
</Tip>
