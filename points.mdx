Points are no longer earned. There is no weekly leaderboard and no new points for trading.

If you earned points under the old program, **Earn** shows them at the bottom of the page as **Past platform points**. That total is only shown when it is above zero.

Rewards now come from [Refer & Earn](/refer-and-earn): a share of trading fees, paid to your Cash.
