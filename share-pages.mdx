# Share pages

Fere AI can publish **public research share pages** at `/share/{id}/…`. These
pages show a question and an AI research answer with sources. They are meant
for discovery (search / social), not as a substitute for your private chats.

## Automated share feed

When enabled by Fere AI ops (feature flag on the Friday backend), a scheduled
job may:

1. Collect recent crypto news and market signals
2. Write research-style questions
3. Answer them with the same research agent stack used in chat
4. Quality-check the answer, then list the page publicly

Automated pages are published under a system account. They are research
summaries with sources — not personalized portfolio advice, and not a signal
that Fere executed a trade for you.

This feed is **off by default**. Turning it on is an environment/ops change,
not a user setting in the app.
